Food heritage is one of the three walk programmes I design and lead, alongside water and urban heritage. Like the others, it has no fixed script: I plan the route around your group and what it wants to understand.

In the Kathmandu Valley, food and water heritage meet — the historic Rajkulo canals once carried water to irrigate royal fields.

Tell me who is coming, roughly when, and what you are curious about, and I will suggest a route.
