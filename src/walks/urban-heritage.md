Urban heritage is the widest of my three walk programmes: the architecture, urban history and living Newar culture of the Valley’s historic towns — the intangible heritage as well as the buildings.

I come to it as a civil engineer. I have assessed earthquake-damaged buildings for the Nepal Reconstruction Authority, given structural support to monumental repoussé sculptures — the Newar craft of hammering metal from the reverse side — with the late master artist Rajkumar Shakya, and advised Kathmandu Metropolitan City on hiti reconstruction. On the walk I bring that eye for how things are built, and how they hold up, to the streets and squares we pass through.

Routes are planned around your group and what it wants to understand. Tell me who is coming and roughly when, and I will suggest one.
