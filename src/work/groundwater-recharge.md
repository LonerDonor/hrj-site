## The brief

Groundwater keeps the Kathmandu Valley’s traditional water systems running — the stone spouts are fed from it — and it is what recharge projects work to replenish.

## What I did

Since 2022 I have worked with the Centre for Integrated Urban Development (CIUD) as consultant engineer and research consultant. I am lead engineer on three active groundwater-recharge projects:

- the core area of Kathmandu Metropolitan City (KMC)
- Balaju–Nagarjun
- Lalitpur Metropolitan City (LMC)

The approach brings traditional water knowledge together with modern hydrogeological methods. I presented research from this work at a CIUD internal conference.

## Where it stands

All three projects are active. Alongside them I presented a paper on groundwater and surface-water interaction in Bara and Rautahat at the 2024 groundwater symposium of the Center of Research for Environment, Energy and Water (CREEW) — it is listed with my [writing and talks](/writing/#creew-groundwater-symposium).

For a municipality, school or institution with land to recharge, this is the work behind a feasibility study: understanding the ground and the water before choosing a design.
