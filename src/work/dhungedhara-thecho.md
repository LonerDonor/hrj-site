## The brief

Dhunge dhara — <span class="deva" lang="ne">ढुङ्गे धारा</span> — is the Nepali name for the stone water spouts called hiti in Newar. In 2023 one was to be constructed in the traditional form in Thecho, and it needed the technical groundwork that any water structure needs before building starts.

## What I did

Working pro bono for the Kathmandu Valley Water Supply Management Board (KVWSB), I prepared that groundwork in full: a site survey, hydraulic discharge calculations, a complete set of computer-aided design (CAD) drawings and a project cost estimate.

## Why I took it on

I did it as a contribution to heritage infrastructure practice. A spout built in the traditional form is still a piece of hydraulic engineering, and it deserves the same rigour as anything else that carries water. It is also the scope I offer anyone restoring or building a spout: survey, calculations, drawings and a costed plan you can take to a builder.
