## The craft

Repoussé is a centuries-old Newar metalworking tradition: the artist shapes sheet metal by hammering it from the reverse side. At the scale of a 40-foot statue, a work of repoussé is also a structure, and it has to be engineered like one.

## What I did

From 2020 to 2021 I worked as civil engineer with Onmetal Art and Craft, Nepal’s largest repoussé workshop, alongside the late master artist Rajkumar Shakya. I provided structural and civil engineering support for two landmark sculptures:

- the **40 ft Little Buddha statue** in Lumbini
- the **74 ft Bhairav face** at Dukuchap, where I worked on the initiation phase

## Why it matters

The work joined structural engineering to a living Newar craft. It is the same meeting of engineering and heritage that runs through my work on water systems: the tradition sets the form, and the engineering has to make that form stand.
