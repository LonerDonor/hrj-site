## The canal

Rajkulo — <span class="deva" lang="ne">राजकुलो</span>, “royal canal” — are the historic canals that once carried water to irrigate royal fields. In Lalitpur the canal is part of the same water heritage as the town’s ponds and stone spouts.

## What I did

I am research lead on a feasibility study for reviving Lalitpur’s Rajkulo. The work documents and maps the historical canal route and its key points, and sets the canal’s heritage significance alongside engineering data. The aim is to connect ancient water infrastructure to the city’s water needs today.

## The question it answers

A feasibility study has to give a straight answer: can a canal built for royal fields be brought back to serve a modern city, and what would it take? Mapping comes first, because every later decision depends on knowing exactly where the canal runs.
