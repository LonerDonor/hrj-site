## The programme

In 2023 Open Learning Exchange (OLE) Nepal ran a robotics pilot in 10 schools. I joined as robotics associate for the pilot, and since 2024 I have led the programme as project coordinator.

## What I did

I designed the robotics teaching and built the tools to go with it: custom interactive interfaces and blocks in mBlock, a block-based coding environment, and Arduino-based modules. I also created an inter-school robotics challenge from scratch.

## What came of it

- The programme has reached more than 300 students across 10+ public schools in Lalitpur.
- The challenge grew into the annual Yala Mayor’s Cup, the programme’s flagship event, supported by KDDI Foundation (Japan). Yala — <span class="deva" lang="new">यल</span> — is the Newar name for Lalitpur.
- OLE Nepal has published a YouTube tutorial series alongside the programme as an open educational resource.

If you run a school, a municipality or an education organisation, I can help with curriculum and lesson plans, teacher training, hands-on workshops and inter-school challenges.
